About

I'm currently an Associate Software Engineer – AI at Techvantage AI in Trivandrum, where I work across the stack that sits between a trained model and a working product: FastAPI services, MongoDB logging, GGUF conversion pipelines, and containerized inference on EC2.

My M.Tech in Artificial Intelligence (APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University, CGPA 9.13) followed a first degree in Mechanical Engineering — a switch that left me with an unusual amount of patience for systems that need to be measured, tuned, and re-measured before they're trusted.

Most of my recent work has centered on making large models practical: fine-tuning LLaMA-3.1 and Mistral-7B for domain-specific tasks, pruning and quantizing them for CPU inference, and building the retrieval and evaluation pipelines that keep them accurate.

  • CPU/GPU inference optimization & GGUF conversion
